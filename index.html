I enjoy understanding how things work. I always have: I take something apart, figure out why it behaves the way it does, and only then put it back together. I am patient with problems that don’t make sense yet, and quick to pick up whatever tool the next one needs. Most of that curiosity ends up in IT: writing code, building apps, the networks and machines they run on, and the security that keeps it all standing.

I study computer science in Ferrara, and whatever I build, I never stop rebuilding. Away from the screen you will find me at the gym, or halfway down some new rabbit hole, in tech or out of it.
